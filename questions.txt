Q. Who received the Nobel Prize in 1901 for 'recognition of the extraordinary services rendered by the discovery of the laws of chemical dynamics and osmotic pressure in solutions'? *(CGL 14 July, 2023 S-1)* 

(a) Hermann Emil Fischer
(b) Jacobus Henricus van't Hoff
(c) Svante August Arrhenius
(d) Henri Moissan
---
Ans. (b)
• In 1901, *Jacobus Henricus van't Hoff* earned the Nobel Prize *in Chemistry* for his groundbreaking discoveries in chemical dynamics and osmotic pressure in solutions.
• In 1902, Hermann Emil Fischer received the Nobel Prize for his research on sugars and purines. Therefore, the correct answer is Jacobus Henricus van't Hoff.

(b) Al - Biruni
(c) Al - Bukari
(d) Al - Khwarizmi
---
Ans. (b)
• The book 'Kitab-ul-Hind' was authored by Al-Biruni.
• Al-Biruni was a *Persian scholar* and polymath during the *Islamic Golden Age*.

Q. Who among the following published a set of Ashokan inscriptions in the year 1877? *(CGL 20 Aug., 2021 S-2)*

(a) DC Sircar
(b) Colin Mackenzie
(c) MS Vats
(d) Alexander Cunningham
---
Ans. (d)
• *Alexander Cunningham* published a set of Ashokan inscriptions *in 1877*.
• He is often referred to as the *"father of Indian archaeology"* and played a pivotal role in the discovery and interpretation of ancient Indian culture.
• He was the *first to excavate* Sarnath (Varanasi).
